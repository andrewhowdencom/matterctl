# Getting a response when needed

Parent: [Household problem](../../PROBLEM.md)

A needed change can be awkward to request, slow to take effect, or forgotten
altogether. A familiar switch press is fine when it works without disrupting
anyone.

## Access to a control

When putting the baby down, the owner needs dimmer lights as a sleep cue and
does not want to stop managing the baby's mood to adjust them. From bed, the
owner may want a light on, off, or dimmed without getting up and dislodging the
blankets. These are examples of costly interruptions, not a requirement to
automate every switch press.

For each situation, count how often someone must stop the ongoing task, move
to a control, or repeat a request. No count has been recorded yet.

## Delay after a request

The owner experienced Google Home light changes as taking seconds. After using
local control, the owner observed physical light changes in roughly 100 ms.
These are informal estimates rather than timed measurements, but they indicate
substantial progress on this part of the problem.

Measure request-to-physical-response time across repeated uses of each
approach; the estimates above are not a timed baseline.

## A missed request

The owner noted that people can forget to make a needed change. We do not yet
have a recurring example and consequence, so this remains a candidate problem.

Once identified, count missed changes per week and the actions needed to
recover; there is no baseline yet.

## More specific problem

- [A device interruption prevents a needed response](switch-control-recovery.md):
  a familiar request can produce no physical response, and an automation may
  remain ineffective after the device returns.

## Measures

For each recurring situation, count interruptions or repeated actions needed
to reach the desired state, missed changes, and the time from a request to the
physical response. A proactive response can remove the request altogether,
provided it fits the situation. The only current timing baseline is the
owner's informal estimate of seconds through Google Home versus roughly
100 ms with local control; the other counts have not been measured.
